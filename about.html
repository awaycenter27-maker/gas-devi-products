An industrial gases business, four decades in

We have spent roughly forty years making and moving industrial gas across West Bengal. The work is practical and the standard is steady: produce reliable oxygen, supply the gases our customers need, and be straightforward about what we can and cannot do.

Who we are

Built on production, trusted on supply

Devi Gas Products is part of a family of businesses based in Kolkata. Our core trade is industrial gas. We manufacture oxygen at our own facilities, and we supply nitrogen and other industrial gases through an established network.

That split matters, and we keep it honest. When we say a gas is manufactured by us, it is produced on our premises. When we say a gas is supplied by us, we are sourcing and delivering it. Customers always know which is which.

Our facilities sit in Howrah and Durgapur, two of the working districts of West Bengal, close to the industries that rely on a steady gas supply.

How we work

What four decades teaches you

  • 01
    Say what is true

    We do not invent capacities, certifications or guarantees. If you ask us something we cannot confirm, we will tell you that rather than guess.

  • 02
    Keep supply steady

    Industry runs on reliability. Manufacturing our own oxygen gives us control over the part of the chain that matters most to our customers.

  • 03
    Answer quickly

    Enquiries get a clear, direct response on what we can supply, in what quantity, and to where.
