The people behind the business

Devi Gas Products is led by two businessmen who pair an established industrial base with a long-term view. One keeps the core trade steady. The other is building toward where the group is headed next.

Established business leadership

Mr. Anirudh Jalan

Mr. Anirudh Jalan is an experienced entrepreneur and business leader with a longstanding involvement in business.

Driven by ambition, a strong business instinct, and a vision for sustainable growth, he brings years of experience and a forward-looking approach to building and leading enterprises.

His leadership reflects a commitment to long-term business development, entrepreneurial thinking, and creating enduring value.

Entrepreneurship and the future of work

Mr. Udbhav Jalan

Mr. Udbhav Jalan is an entrepreneur and business leader with over eight years of experience building businesses, workspaces, communities, and organizational systems.
